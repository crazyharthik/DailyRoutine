Section 1

Skin Care Routine

All-natural. Fixes pigmentation, tanning, open pores & dark spots.

🌅
Morning Routine (7:00–7:15 AM)
Daily • Takes 15 min
1
Cold water face wash

Splash cold water 10–15 times. Tightens pores instantly. NO hot water on face — ever.

2
Ice Therapy (Ice Globe / Ice Cube)

Wrap 1–2 ice cubes in a thin cloth. Gently glide in circular motions over cheeks, forehead, jaw for 3–5 min. Reduces pores, puffiness, and brightens tone.

3
Raw Aloe Vera Gel

Apply a thin layer of pure aloe gel (from plant or store). Leave it on. Works as a natural moisturiser + pigmentation fighter.

4
Sunscreen (natural option)

Apply coconut oil + a pinch of turmeric (tiny amount). Or use aloe vera as a light SPF barrier if stepping out briefly.

🌙
Night Routine (10:00–10:15 PM)
Daily • Repair while you sleep
1
Raw milk cleanse

Dip a cotton ball in cold raw milk. Wipe entire face. Removes pollution, dead skin, and unclogs pores. Wait 2 min, rinse.

2
Potato juice toner

Grate a small potato, squeeze juice. Apply with cotton on dark spots and pigmented areas. Leave 10 min. Rinse. Best natural de-pigmentation agent.

3
Overnight oil treatment

1–2 drops of pure castor oil + 3 drops almond oil. Mix in palm. Apply lightly over face. Rich in Vitamin E. Wake up with soft skin.

🧪
Weekly Face Pack (Sun & Wed)
2× per week • Leave 20 min
Glow Pack – For Pigmentation & Tanning
1
Ingredients

2 tsp besan (gram flour) + ½ tsp turmeric + 1 tsp curd + 1 tsp lemon juice + few drops of rose water. Mix into smooth paste.

2
Apply & wait

Apply evenly on face and neck. Leave 20–25 min till it dries. Rinse with cold water in circular motions.

3
Alternate Pack – Tan Removal

Tomato pulp + cucumber juice + 1 tsp honey. Apply. 20 min. Rinse. Great for suntan and uneven tone.

Scrub (Every Saturday) – Open Pores
1
Coffee + honey scrub

1 tsp used coffee grounds + 1 tsp raw honey + few drops of coconut oil. Gently scrub in circles for 2 min. Rinse. Clears dead cells, opens pores, brightens.

2
Sugar + lemon scrub (alternate week)

1 tsp raw sugar + ½ tsp lemon juice + 1 tsp olive oil. Gentle circular scrub. Bleaches dark spots naturally.

❄️
Ice Therapy – Daily Protocol
Morning must-do • 5 min
Never apply ice directly. Always wrap in a thin muslin cloth or cotton.
Motion: Gentle circles starting from chin → cheeks → nose sides → forehead → temples.
Frequency: Every morning. Optional: after gym/exercise to reduce sweat-pores.
Power-up: Freeze green tea or rose water as ice cubes for added skin benefits.
Results visible in: 3–4 weeks of consistent use. Pores visibly tighter by week 2.

Internal Detox Plan

Clean your liver, gut, and digestion — naturally from inside out.

Drink on empty stomach

Lifestyle Rules

Energy, focus, quitting smoking — step by step.

🚭
Quit Smoking — 8-Week Plan
Step by step. Realistic. No cold turkey shock.
Week 1–2: Awareness
Start here
Track every cigarette. Count how many daily. Write the time and trigger. Don't cut yet — just become aware. Replace 1 cigarette per day with a walk.
Week 3–4: Reduce by 40%
Cutting
If you smoke 10/day → bring to 6. Delay first cigarette by 1 hour after waking. Chew saunf or cardamom instead. Drink cold water when craving hits.
Week 5–6: Reduce by 70%
Almost there
Max 3 per day. No smoking indoors or near workspace. Identify your 3 biggest triggers. Replace each with a substitute habit (gum, cold water, 5 push-ups).
Week 7–8: Quit
Freedom
Max 1/day → then 0. When craving hits: drink water → do 10 push-ups → walk 5 min. Craving lasts only 3–5 min. Outlast it.
Natural Smoking Craving Killers
Cardamom (elaichi) Saunf (fennel) Cold water Mulethi (licorice) Ginger tea 5 deep breaths 10 push-ups
⚡
Energy & Focus Rules (Developer Life)
Sustainably sharp without caffeine dependency
No chai/coffee before 9 AM. Let cortisol peak naturally first. First drink = water or lemon water.
Max 2 cups black coffee per day. No chai — hidden sugar destroys energy cycles.
Stand up every 45 min. Set a timer. 2-min walk breaks restore focus better than coffee.
Soak 5 almonds + 2 walnuts overnight. Eat at breakfast. Brain fuel for coding sessions.
3 PM energy crash cure: Cold water on face + 20 jumping jacks. Never another coffee after 3 PM.
Phone in another room during work blocks. Every notification resets 23 min of focus.
📋
Daily Consistency Rules
The non-negotiables
Zero sugar — always. Not "mostly." Check biscuit ingredients. Sugar is hiding in everything.
3L+ water daily. Keep a 1L bottle visible on your desk. Refill 3× a day.
Sleep before 11 PM. Results depend more on sleep than on workouts.
10-min post-lunch walk. This one habit alone reduces belly fat accumulation significantly.
Finish dinner by 8 PM. Late eating = fat storage. No exceptions.
Track weeks, not days. Miss a day → get back on track next morning. Never skip 2 days in a row.
